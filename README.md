<h1>🔢 Guess My Number – Challenge Your Intuition!</h1>
Step into the world of mystery and logic with the Guess My Number game — a simple yet addictive number guessing challenge that tests your brainpower and instinct!

🎯 The computer secretly selects a random number between 1 and 20, and your mission is to guess it in the fewest tries possible. Each time you guess, you'll get feedback — "Too high" or "Too low" — helping you zero in on the correct number.

🧠 It's a game of deduction, focus, and a bit of luck. Keep your streak alive and beat your high score!

✨ Features:

Random number changes every time you restart.

Real-time feedback on your guesses.

Score and high score tracking to keep you motivated.

Sleek, responsive interface built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Great for sharpening your logical thinking and number sense.

<h1>🎉 Whether you're taking a quick break or trying to beat your own record, Guess My Number is a perfect pick-me-up game to train your brain and have fun!</h1>
